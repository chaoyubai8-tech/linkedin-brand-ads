开场 / Opening

Hi, we’re Tripo.

How will you turn a growing character and prop list into assets for the game?

Tripo Studio Team Plan supports your game project:
• Generate editable 3D characters and props from text or images.
• Continue in Blender, Unity or Unreal with your team's checks.
• Keep assets in a shared workspace for review and reuse.

你好，我们是 Tripo。

角色与道具清单不断增长，怎样把这些需求变成游戏项目能用的资产？

Tripo Studio 团队版支持游戏项目的资产制作与使用：
• 从文字或图片生成可继续编辑的 3D 角色与道具。

Continue in Blender, Unity or Unreal. Your team checks materials, animation and in-game performance before use. Keep reviewed assets in the shared library.

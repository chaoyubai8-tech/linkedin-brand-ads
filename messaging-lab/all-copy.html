回复 1 / Response 1

Use single-image or supported multi-view generation to create a 3D product model. Add PBR textures, with an optional 8K tier for material detail in close-up content.

Retrieve GLB, or convert the model to USDZ. Review it against the real product, then reuse the approved asset in your own interactive product display. Your team implements the viewer and display integration.

Open Quick Start to plan the API calls.

回复 2 / Response 2

Combine the API functions your product content needs:
• Image or multi-view generation for a 3D model.
• PBR textures, including 8K options for close-up views.
• GLB output or USDZ conversion for display formats.

Your developers use approved assets in their own content platform. Your team controls product checks, the viewer and where content is published.

8K options cost extra credits. Start with Quick Start.
